🍌 Banana Poi (Poi Fa'i)

A classic Samoan sweet treat made with ripe bananas and coconut milk.

Ingredients

  • 6–8 very ripe bananas (mash fine)
  • 1 can coconut milk (or fresh coconut cream)
  • 1–2 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1 lemon leaf or lemon zest (optional, for fragrance)
  • Cold water or ice cubes

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mash the ripe bananas thoroughly until smooth.
  2. Mix in the coconut milk and sugar until well combined.
  3. Add cold water/ice to achieve your preferred liquid consistency.
  4. Chill in the fridge before serving cold!

🍍 Pai Fala (Half-Moon Pineapple Pie)

Delicious traditional Samoan pineapple custard folded into a half-moon pie shell.

Ingredients

  • 1 can crushed pineapple (with juice)
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch (dissolved in a little water)
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • Basic pie crust dough

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan, combine crushed pineapple, sugar, and coconut milk over medium heat.
  2. Stir in the cornstarch mixture and cook until thickened into a custard texture. Let cool.
  3. Roll out pie dough into circles, place pineapple filling on one half, and fold over into a half-moon shape.
  4. Seal edges, crimp with a fork, and cut small steam vents on top.
  5. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25–30 minutes until golden brown.
